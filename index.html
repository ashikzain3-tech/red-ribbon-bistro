Red Ribbon Bakery LLC bakes in the UAE for families who want fresh bread and pastries on the table. From everyday sliced loaves to Filipino favourites like pandesal, Spanish roll and hopia, every pack carries the ribbon and the promise on our logo: baked with passion.
